Dissecting LLM News Sentiment Scores
Identity Priors, Batch Composition, Scoring Noise, and Price Echoes in Return Predictability
A measurement audit on 36 large U.S. equities over July–October 2026. The same headlines scored under conditions that each vary one ingredient—identity visible, removed, or swapped, repeat calls, controlled batch composition, temperature-zero decoding, and the dictionary—then re-scored in pre-specified staged sessions reveal a marginal baseline whose significance depends on the scoring draw, a reliable but stochastic instrument, a mild negative identity prior masked by session drift and mostly carried by batch composition, predictive content directionally concentrated in price-describing headlines, and a sample selected on realized volatility.
